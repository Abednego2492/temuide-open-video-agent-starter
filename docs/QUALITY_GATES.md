# Quality gates

1. Asset provenance and readable media.
2. Accurate speech boundaries and complete meaning.
3. Reference interpretation with evidence.
4. One dominant viewer focus per beat.
5. Mobile-safe typography, geometry and contrast.
6. Contextually valid B-roll, no fictional claims.
7. Motion continuity and deliberate timing.
8. Original dialogue clear; no unwanted voice overlap.
9. Alpha/foreground edges and product colors preserved.
10. Real MP4 created and probed; watch the entire result.

`npm run critic` covers **only basic technical checks**. A human or multimodal critic must inspect real frames and audio. All automatic PASS states must link to evidence. Preserve timestamped defect reports and rerender after corrections.
