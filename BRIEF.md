# Edit brief

## My assets
Describe what's in `public/assets/`, or ask the agent to inspect.

## References
Place your reference videos/photos in `public/references/`.

## Goal
Make a short vertical video that clearly communicates my message.

## Audience and platform
Instagram Reels, TikTok, or YouTube Shorts. Set the target based on actual use.

## Creative guidance
Reference-driven, readable typography, intentional B-roll, minimal competing motion, credible product evidence.

## Duration
Agent recommends a duration based on available footage and message.

## Audio
Keep original voice clear. Use only licensed audio.

## Output
`output/final.mp4` with `output/critic-report.json` and an editable Remotion project.

## Constraints
No fake stats, no irrelevant stock footage, no broken phrase cuts, no mandatory fixed editing style.
